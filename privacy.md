SecOpsNews is run by [thechelsuk](https://thechels.uk). This notice covers <{{ site.url }}> and its RSS feed.

## The short version

- No cookies, no accounts, no ads tracking.
- Cookieless analytics give us aggregate numbers only.
- We never sell or share visitor data, including with sponsors.

## Analytics

We use [Ahrefs Web Analytics](https://ahrefs.com/web-analytics) to see how the site is used overall, for example page views, referring sites, country, and browser or device type. It doesn't set cookies or store anything on your device, and it doesn't build a profile of you. See the [Ahrefs privacy policy](https://ahrefs.com/privacy).

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address to run and secure the service. See the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Links and sponsors

Headlines link to other websites, which have their own privacy policies. Sponsor links add `?r=thechelsuk` so sponsors can see visits came from SecOpsNews; nothing about you is passed on.

## If you contact us

If you email or use the contact form, we use your message and details only to reply, and don't add you to any list.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. [Contact us](/contact/) to do this, or complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).
