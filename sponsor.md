SecOpsNews has one sponsor at a time. Your card sits near the top of the dashboard, the full width of the page, for the whole of your booking. It shows your square logo, a title, a one-line description and a link to your site, labelled "Sponsored by".

The audience is engineering leaders, security professionals and people who look after infrastructure, checking the day's security news, advisories and outages.

## Available slots

Bookings are by the calendar month, with a minimum of one month.

## How to book

Email [secops@thechels.uk](mailto:secops@thechels.uk?subject=Sponsoring%20SecOpsNews) with:

- the month or months you'd like
- your company name and the link to use
- a title of up to 60 characters
- a one-line description of up to 140 characters
- a square logo, at least 96 × 96 pixels

Pricing is on application.

## Sponsorship terms {#terms}

1. **Minimum booking.** The minimum booking is one calendar month. A month runs from the 1st to the last day of that month, UK time.
2. **Price and payment.** Pricing is on application. Payment is in advance through Stripe, and a slot is only confirmed once payment has been received.
3. **No refunds.** Payments are non-refundable, including if you cancel or ask for your card to be removed early.
4. **One sponsor at a time.** Your card is the only sponsorship shown on the dashboard during your booking.
5. **Content.** You confirm you have the right to use the logo, name and text you supply. Sponsorships that are misleading, unlawful or unrelated to the audience may be declined or removed.
6. **Labelling.** Sponsored cards are labelled "Sponsored by" and the link is marked as sponsored for search engines.
7. **No guarantees.** No particular number of views or clicks is guaranteed. SecOpsNews uses cookieless [Ahrefs Web Analytics](https://ahrefs.com/web-analytics) for aggregate site statistics; it doesn't track individual visitors or share visitor data with sponsors (see the [privacy notice](/privacy/)). Your link includes `?r=thechelsuk` so you can see referrals in your own analytics.
8. **Downtime.** If the site is unavailable for an extended period during your booking for reasons on our side, the missed time will be credited as extra days, not refunded.
9. **Changes.** These terms may change, but changes won't affect bookings that have already been paid for.
